Interpretation

Final result

Five predicted complexes were delivered with the 218-residue protein and supplied ligand. The report places them in the same catalytic cleft and describes only minor conformational differences.

Site and orientation. The reported contact residues include 19, 22–26, 61–72, 136 and 162–165. Cavity-placement tests support the cleft within the reported computational procedure. Tail variation remains 0.6–1.4 Å, while the terminal carboxyl group and leucine side chain remain within 1 Å across aligned structures.

Revision decision. The report describes a 2.2–2.45 Å sulfur–carbon distance and approximately 1 Å of overlap. Its five acceptance conditions were: separation ≥3.2 Å, overlap <0.6 Å, Cys25 contact within 4.5 Å, retention of contacts with residues 19 and 164, and at least 15 interface contacts. No attempted revision met all conditions, so the original conformations were retained.

Confidence and limitations. The report cites AF3Score ipTM values around 0.94–0.97. These are model-confidence values, not experimental accuracy. The tail orientation and cysteine overlap remain unresolved. A covalent-adduct explanation was suggested but not tested; literature evidence was limited to family-level abstracts and fragments. Only CIF/mmCIF files were delivered.
